# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Romanas is an adult beginner learning on a Casio CT-S1 or similar MIDI keyboard. He practices at home with a computer near the piano and wants to understand the structure of music, not only reproduce note sequences.

## Product Purpose

Cadence turns daily piano practice into a clear loop: connect, understand, play, receive specific feedback, and practice the weakest movement again. Success means Romanas can learn foundational classical repertoire while building enough theory fluency to improvise and compose with intention.

## Brand Personality

Calm, intelligent, encouraging. The voice is concise and observant, like a thoughtful teacher sitting beside the piano.

## Anti-references

Avoid noisy gamification, childish streak mechanics, neon music-production aesthetics, generic AI dashboards, dense notation software, and lesson apps that reduce music to pressing a glowing key.

## Design Principles

1. Put the next musical action at the center of the screen.
2. Explain the musical reason close to the note or chord being practiced.
3. Turn mistakes into small, specific drills without shame or drama.
4. Keep progress legible, grounded, and local to the learner.
5. Preserve familiar controls so attention stays on listening and playing.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA contrast and focus visibility. Never rely on color alone for note states or feedback. Support keyboard and touch input, reduced motion, responsive layouts, and a complete simulator when Web MIDI is unavailable.
